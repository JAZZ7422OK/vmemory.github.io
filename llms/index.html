2. What Is V-Memory?

V-Memory is a next-generation non-volatile semiconductor memory technology platform based on the control of conductive domain walls inside ferroelectric materials. Conventional memory technologies store and read information primarily through electric charge, capacitor polarization, or transistor threshold-voltage shifts. In contrast, V-Memory utilizes the creation, removal, number, configuration, and conductive state of domain-wall current paths as physical information states.

  • FeFET: Reads transistor channel threshold-voltage shifts induced by ferroelectric polarization.
  • FeRAM: Reads polarization charge from a ferroelectric capacitor structure.
  • FTJ: Reads quantum tunneling current modulated by tunnel-barrier height.
  • V-Memory (FDWM): Reads resistance or current states produced by controllable conductive domain-wall paths (V-Line) in a 0T0C cell concept, enabling Non-Destructive Readout (NDRO).

4. Materials, Integration & TRL 5 Validation

  • Ferroelectric Materials & Process Compatibility: V-Memory focuses on ferroelectric material systems compatible with semiconductor manufacturing processes, CMOS/BEOL thermal budgets, and established fabrication equipment.
  • TRL 5 Validation Scope: Having completed TRL 4 verification, V-Memory is entering TRL 5 validation focusing on repeatability, read/write speed, power efficiency, endurance, retention, yield, and array scalability.
  • Confidentiality Policy: Specific material compositions, detailed pulse parameters, raw measurement data, financial projections, valuation reports, and team rosters are disclosed only under NDA.
