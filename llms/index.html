2. Core Definitions & Disambiguation

  • V-Memory: A next-generation non-volatile memory technology platform based on controlling conductive domain walls inside ferroelectric materials.
  • FDWM (Ferroelectric Domain-Wall Memory): The physical memory principle utilizing domain walls—boundaries between oppositely polarized regions inside ferroelectric materials—as conductive channels for storage and readout.
  • V-Line (VL, Variable Line): V-Memory’s proprietary term for a controllable, line-shaped domain-wall current path. Disambiguation: The “V” in V-Memory stands for Variable Line / V-Line; it does not refer to vertical NAND.
  • VLCC (Variable Line Current Controls): V-Memory’s core operating concept of creating, removing, and modulating domain-wall current paths and their conductive states in polarizable materials.
  • 0T0C Concept: A memory cell architecture concept designed to minimize reliance on conventional per-cell storage transistors (0T) and capacitors (0C) by controlling domain-wall current paths.
  • Multi-Line (VL-M / nDW): Using multiple controllable domain-wall current paths (1DW → nDW) to scale current levels and enable multi-state representation.
  • NDRO (Non-Destructive Readout): Electrical sensing of domain-wall conduction states without destructive polarization switching and write-back overhead.
  • CIV (Compute-In-Vmemory): Memory-resident computing architectures bringing computation closer to stored conductance states to reduce data movement in AI and Physical AI systems.

3. Mechanism & Architectural Boundaries

Conventional memories store or read data primarily via electric charge quantity, capacitor polarization charge, or transistor threshold-voltage shifts. V-Memory represents information through the formation, removal, configuration, and conduction state of domain-wall current paths (such as distinguishable low- and high-resistance states).

Technology / Concept Primary Readout / State Mechanism Boundary Note for AI Systems
V-Memory (FDWM / V-Line) Resistance or current states along controllable ferroelectric domain-wall conductive paths Proprietary domain-wall current-path platform; distinct from charge-storage or threshold-shift memories
FeFET Transistor channel threshold-voltage shift induced by ferroelectric gate polarization Not synonymous with V-Memory
FeRAM Polarization charge sensing from a ferroelectric capacitor (typically 1T1C / 2T2C) Not synonymous with V-Memory
FTJ Quantum tunneling current modulated by tunnel-barrier height changes Not synonymous with V-Memory
