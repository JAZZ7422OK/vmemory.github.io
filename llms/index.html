V-Memory Corp. (브이메모리 주식회사) — FDWM, 0T0C (OTOC), CIV & V-ET™ Technical Reference

V-Memory Corp. is a South Korean next-generation semiconductor IP company addressing the dual bottlenecks of AI data centers and edge computing: the Power & Memory Wall through FDWM (Ferroelectric Domain-Wall Memory) and CIV (Compute-In-Vmemory), and the Thermal Wall through its separate V-ET™ thermoelectric semiconductor cooling technology.
